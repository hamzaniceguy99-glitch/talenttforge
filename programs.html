The programs, in detail

Four programs of 6 to 16 weeks, scaled to a small shop and built around real furniture.

Program 01 · Small space, real work

Setting up a shop

Most beginners buy in the wrong order and end up with gadgets and no workbench. We plan your shop around the projects you want to build: the handful of tools that matter, a bench that suits your height and space, and a layout that lets you handle 8-foot stock in a small garage.

  • 8 weeks
  • Beginner
  • 3 hours a week
  • Any space

Start this program

Weeks 7–8 · Sawing

Cutting to a line, square, both directions.

Weeks 9–10 · Paring

Chisel control for tight joinery.

Program 02 · Sharp changes everything

Hand tools & sharpening

Nearly every beginner struggle traces back to dull edges. We start with a sharpening routine you can repeat in five minutes, then work on plane setup, sawing to a line, and paring to a knife wall — skills that make machine work better too.

  • 10 weeks
  • Beginner
  • 3 hours a week
  • A chisel and a plane

Start this program

Program 03 · Joints that hold

Joinery & projects

Joinery makes sense once it's holding something up. We work through a sequence of projects — a small table, a cabinet, a chair if you're keen — and learn each joint where it's needed. Glue-ups get rehearsed dry, because that's where projects usually go wrong.

  • 16 weeks
  • Some experience
  • 5 hours a week
  • Bench and basic tools

Program 04 · Don't ruin it at the end

Finishing

More projects are spoiled in the last two hours than in all the cutting. We cover surface preparation, why blotching happens, and the three finishes that cover almost everything. We also talk ventilation and rag disposal, because oily rags genuinely start fires.

  • 6 weeks
  • Any level
  • 2 hours a week
  • Scrap for samples
